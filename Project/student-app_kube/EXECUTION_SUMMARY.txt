Q: How do I access the app?
A: Run "minikube service nginx-service" or visit http://192.168.49.2:30080

Q: Where is the data stored?
A: In MySQL database on PersistentVolume (survives pod restarts)

Q: How many Flask replicas are running?
A: 2 replicas for high availability and load balancing

Q: Can I scale the app?
A: Yes, edit k8s/03-app-deployment.yaml and change replicas count

Q: How do I view logs?
A: kubectl logs -f deployment/<component-name>

Q: How do I restart everything?
A: kubectl delete -f k8s/ && kubectl apply -f k8s/
